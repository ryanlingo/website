I'm **Ryan Lingo**, an applied AI research engineer and developer advocate at [99P Labs](https://www.99plabs.com/), the Ohio office of [Honda Research Institute](https://usa.honda-ri.com/). I write about AI, about how people and teams learn, and about how to solve open-ended problems, and my background in philosophy shapes how I approach all three. I revise pages over time rather than posting once and moving on, so each page lists when I started it, when I last changed it, how finished it is, and how confident I am in it.
