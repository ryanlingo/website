This is the personal website of Ryan Lingo. I'm an applied AI research engineer and developer advocate at [99P Labs](https://www.99plabs.com/), and the site holds my writing, my research papers, and my talks. It's modeled on [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/about), so the pages are long pieces that I keep revising, and not posts that I write once and leave. For more about me, see [[me|Me]].

## What I write about

Most of my writing is about AI and about how people and teams solve open-ended problems. On the AI side, I write about how to use AI well, what information an AI system needs to see, and where human judgment still matters, e.g. in [[you-are-not-using-ai-enough|You Are Not Using AI Enough]] and [[the-curation-problem|The Curation Problem]]. On the problem-solving side, I write about how learning works in practice and how to keep research tied to real problems, e.g. in [[what-do-we-mean-by-learning|What Do We Mean by Learning?]] and [[avoiding-research-traps|Avoiding Research Traps]].

My background is in philosophy, and it shapes how I write about technology. Inspired by John Dewey, I see technology as the tools people invent and use to resolve problems they've run into, so I try to start from the problem and judge each tool by whether it helps.

## How the site is organized

The links at the top of each page lead to the main sections:

- **Site** is this page.
- **[[me|Me]]** gives my background and how to contact me.
- **[[research/index|Research]]** describes my research areas and lists my papers.
- **[[blog/index|Blog]]** holds my writing, newest first.
- **[[speaking|Speaking]]** lists the topics I speak on and where I've given talks.
- **[[links|Links]]** points to my profiles on other sites.

The [[index|homepage]] groups my writing by topic, and [[new|New]] lists the pages I've changed most recently. [[logs/index|Logs]] holds plain records, e.g. the [[site-changes|site changelog]]. The site also has sections for [[notes/index|notes]] and [[reviews/index|reviews]], which are empty for now.

Many of the blog posts first appeared on the [99P Labs blog](https://medium.com/99p-labs), and each of those posts ends with a link to the original. The versions here are the ones I keep revising.

## Page metadata

Each page has the following metadata under its title:

- **Created** is the date I started the page, and **modified** is the date of the last real change.
- **Status** says how finished the page is, and it goes from `notes` to `draft` to `in progress` to `finished`.
- **Confidence** says how likely I think the page's main claims are, using the words certain, highly likely, likely, possible, unlikely, remote, and impossible.
- **Importance** is a score from 0 to 10 for how much I think the topic matters.
