AI is changing how we work, learn, and make decisions. My talks help audiences use these tools well, think clearly when the answer isn't known, and leave with ideas they can put to work the next day.

I'm available for keynotes, conferences, guest lectures, workshops, and panels, as well as podcast and online interviews. I've spoken to student, academic, and professional audiences, from university classrooms to company teams and hackathons.

## Some suggested topics

These are a few of the areas I often speak about, and I'm happy to shape a talk around your audience and event.

- **Working with AI.** How people, teams, and organizations can get more out of AI, and where human judgment still matters. See [[blog/you-are-not-using-ai-enough|You Are Not Using AI Enough]].
- **Learning and uncertainty.** How people and teams make progress when the problem isn't clear yet. See [[blog/learning-through-uncertainty|Learning Through Uncertainty]] and [[blog/what-do-we-mean-by-learning|What Do We Mean by Learning?]].
- **Data and decisions.** How organizations turn what they know into what they do. See [[blog/why-we-have-the-data|Why "We Have the Data" Is Never the Same as "We Know What to Do"]].
- **Knowledge and AI systems.** How AI systems find, use, and keep track of information. See [[blog/the-curation-problem|The Curation Problem]].
- **Thinking about the future.** How to reason, predict, and make choices when no one knows what comes next.
