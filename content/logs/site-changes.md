## 2026-11-05

- **New pages**
  - Added the [[speaking|Speaking]] page, which lists the topics I speak on and the places I've given talks.
  - Added the [[research/index|Research]] page in place of the Syllabi section. It describes my four research areas, i.e. forward deployed engineering, synthetic data, context engineering, and evaluation, and lists my papers, with a diagram for each area.
  - Added two older posts from the 99P Labs blog, [[avoiding-research-traps|Avoiding Research Traps]] and [[building-resilient-ai-with-modular-output-decomposition|Building Resilient AI with Modular Output Decomposition]], with their diagrams redrawn.
  - Started this changelog.
- **Changed pages**
  - Added my background to the [[me|Me]] page, set up the anonymous feedback form, and changed the contact email to my Gmail address, hidden from web crawlers.
  - Added LinkedIn, X, Google Scholar, and 99P Labs to the [[links|Links]] page.
  - Rewrote the [[about|Site]] page to match what's on the site.
  - Rewrote the homepage introduction, and added a Research list that links to my four research areas.
  - Rewrote the summaries under the page titles on the homepage and the Me, Research, Speaking, and Links pages in first person, to match the rest of the site.
- **Site design**
  - Changed the header links to Site, Me, Research, Blog, Speaking, and Links. The [[new|New]] page is still linked from the homepage and the Site page.
  - Links to other sites now open in a new tab.
  - The anonymous feedback links in the footer, on the homepage, and on the Site page now go straight to the Google Form.
  - Body text is now left aligned, because justified text left wide gaps between words.
  - The email address on the Speaking page is hidden from web crawlers.
- **Search and link previews**
  - Page titles in search results and browser tabs now end with my name.
  - Each page now tells search engines and AI tools who wrote it, and blog posts list their publication dates.
  - Redesigned the link preview images. Each one shows a large RL logo on a color for its section, e.g. red for blog posts and blue for research, next to the page title and description.
  - Link preview images no longer show tags, and they're PNG files so more apps show them.
  - Added a robots.txt file that points search engines to the sitemap, and an [llms.txt](https://ryanlingo.com/llms.txt) file that guides AI tools to the main pages and posts.
